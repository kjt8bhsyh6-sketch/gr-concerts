The short version

We collect as little as possible. There are no accounts and no ads, and right now we use no tracking cookies or analytics.

Email alerts (Buttondown)

If you sign up for email alerts, your email address goes to Buttondown, the service that sends our newsletter. We use it only to send you concert updates. We never sell or share it. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can ask us to delete your address at any time.

"I'm going" and "Interested" picks

Listings you mark are saved only in your own browser (localStorage) and are never sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no tracking cookies and runs no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (see GitHub's privacy statement).

Affiliate links in the future: some ticket links may become affiliate links. When you click one, the ticket seller or affiliate partner may set its own cookies on its site to credit us for the referral. Those cookies are covered by that partner's privacy policy, not ours.

Other sites

Ticket, venue and calendar links go to other websites, each with its own privacy policy. If we turn on optional features like an event-submission form (Google Forms) or comments (giscus/GitHub), those services handle what you choose to send them.
