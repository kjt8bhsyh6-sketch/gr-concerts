The short version

Grand Rapids Happenings is operated by The Canasta Club. We collect as little as possible. Profiles are optional. We use no tracking or advertising cookies; the only cookie we set is a sign-in cookie for people who create a profile and sign in.

Email alerts (Buttondown)

If you sign up for email alerts, your email address goes to Buttondown, the service that sends our newsletter. We use it only to send you concert updates. We never sell or share it. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can ask us to delete your address at any time.

Optional profiles

You can use the whole site without a profile. If you create one, we store:

  • Your email address, used to send your sign-in link and, only if you turn alerts on, a "Your Grand Rapids picks this week" email listing upcoming events that match your picks. Alert emails are sent from grhappenings@gmail.com (Google Gmail). Every alert email has a one-click link to turn alerts off, and you can also switch them off in your profile.
  • Your picks: the genres, venues, artists, teams, game types and seasonal event types you choose, plus your alert setting.
  • When the profile was created, last saved and last signed in.
  • A short-lived sign-in link token (expires after 15 minutes or once used) and a sign-in session token (stays active until you sign out or delete your account).

No password is stored. This data lives on Cloudflare (Workers KV), the service that runs our profile server. We never sell or share it. Sign-in emails are sent through an email service (Google Gmail, from grhappenings@gmail.com) that receives only your address and the link. If it is unavailable, a backup email service (Mailjet) may be used instead. Your browser keeps your sign-in session and a copy of your picks in the grh_s cookie and browser storage (localStorage / IndexedDB; see Cookies below) so you stay signed in across visits and home-screen app launches.

To delete it: sign in, tap your initial under the Concerts / Sports / Seasonal toggle, then tap Delete my account. Your profile and email are removed right away. Or email us at the address below and we'll delete it for you.

"I'm going" and "Interested" picks

Listings you mark are saved only in your own browser (localStorage) and are never sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Cookies, local storage and analytics

Sign-in cookie. If you sign in to a profile, our profile server sets one cookie named grh_s. It holds your sign-in session so you stay signed in until you sign out. It is HttpOnly (page scripts cannot read it), Secure (sent only over HTTPS), and SameSite=Lax (Path=/). It is renewed automatically while you use the site; signing out removes it. If you never sign in, this cookie is never set. It is not used for tracking or advertising.

Browser storage (localStorage and IndexedDB). Your browser also keeps your sign-in session token, a copy of your picks, and any "I'm going" / "Interested" marks in localStorage and IndexedDB so you stay signed in across visits and home-screen app launches. Signing out removes your sign-in session and profile from all of them; clearing your browser data removes all local copies.

Analytics. We use GoatCounter and Cloudflare Web Analytics to count anonymous page views and button clicks. Both are cookieless: they set no cookies and do not build profiles of you. The site and our profile server are hosted on Cloudflare (Cloudflare Pages and Cloudflare Workers), which may keep standard server logs (see Cloudflare's privacy policy). Our old address on GitHub Pages only forwards visitors here; GitHub may keep standard server logs for those visits (see GitHub's privacy statement).

Affiliate links. Some ticket links may be affiliate links. When you click one, the ticket seller or affiliate network may set its own cookies on its site to credit us for the referral. Those cookies are covered by that partner's privacy policy, not ours. We don't share your name or email with them. See our affiliate disclosure.

Other sites

Ticket, venue and calendar links go to other websites, each with its own privacy policy. If we turn on optional features like an event-submission form (Google Forms) or comments (giscus/GitHub), those services handle what you choose to send them.

Children under 13

Grand Rapids Happenings is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and if we learn that we have, we will delete it right away.

Questions

Email grhappenings@gmail.com. If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top.
