GR Happenings Support

Need help with the GR Happenings iPhone app or grhappenings.com? Found a listing that's wrong, or have a question? We're happy to help.

How to get help

Email us at grhappenings@gmail.com. Tell us what you were doing, what you expected, and, for the app, your iPhone model and iOS version.

For event details such as times, tickets, and cancellations, always confirm with the venue or ticket seller, since they have the final word.

About the app

The app is GR Happenings, an iPhone app for concerts, sports, and seasonal events around Grand Rapids. It is operated by The Canasta Club LLC, which owns the GR Happenings name and app. William Jenkins, a member of the LLC, publishes it on the App Store with the authorization of the LLC's members, and the App Store lists him as the seller.
