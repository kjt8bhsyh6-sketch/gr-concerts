What this is

Grand Rapids Happenings is a free, hand-made guide to what's going on around town: concerts, sports, and seasonal events within about 25 miles of Grand Rapids. We check every listing, update the site daily, and link you to the official ticket seller or box office so you can get tickets straight from the source.

Who runs it

Grand Rapids Happenings is run by The Canasta Club, a group of Grand Rapids-area locals and friends who love a good night out and wanted one simple place to see what's coming up.

Affiliate disclosure

Disclosure: Some ticket links on Grand Rapids Happenings are affiliate links. If you buy through them, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. It helps keep this guide free. We list shows based on what's happening in GR, not on who pays us.
