GR Happenings App Privacy Policy

The GR Happenings iPhone app ("the app") is operated by The Canasta Club LLC, a Michigan limited liability company ("we," "us," "our"), which owns the GR Happenings name, website, app, and related intellectual property. The app is published on the App Store by William Jenkins, a member of The Canasta Club LLC, with the authorization of the LLC's members. Because he publishes it through his individual Apple developer account, the App Store lists William Jenkins as the seller. This policy explains what the app does with information. It covers the app only. Our website, grhappenings.com, has its own privacy policy at grhappenings.com/privacy.

In short: the app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking, and we do not collect personal information through the app.

1. Information we do not collect

You don't sign up or sign in. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, calendar entries, location, or payment information through the app. The app does not use advertising identifiers, analytics tools, crash-reporting services, or third-party tracking software, and it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

2. Information that stays on your iPhone

The app stores the following only on your device: your interests (such as music, venues, artists, food, drinks, coffee, and dessert), saved events and favorites, notification settings, the reservation records you choose to save in Bookings, and cached event descriptions, venue locations, and weather. We cannot see this information. Deleting the app deletes it.

3. Permissions the app may ask for

The app works if you deny every permission. You can change or withdraw any permission at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

  • Location (While Using the App): to show where you are on the event map and, if you turn on "Leave now" reminders, to estimate your drive time to a saved event. The app does not use background location. Drive times and directions are calculated by Apple Maps, so your location is processed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We do not receive or store your location.
  • Calendar: to check which evenings are busy so "Free nights" can show when you're open, and to add events or plans you choose using the standard iPhone calendar screen. Your calendar is read on your iPhone only and is never sent to us.
  • Notifications: reminders you turn on are scheduled on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to a server.

4. Services the app connects to

To show listings and details, the app downloads public information over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. These requests do not include personal information about you, but like any internet request, the service you connect to can see your device's IP address and basic technical details, such as device type.

  • grhappenings.com (our event feed): the public list of events. If you turn on "New matches," the app also checks this feed in the background from time to time. Our hosting provider may briefly process IP addresses and standard request details to deliver and secure the service. We do not use them to identify or track app users.
  • Event websites: the public page for an event, to show a short description.
  • Wikipedia (Wikimedia Foundation): public summaries about performers or teams, using names from our event list.
  • Apple (iTunes Search and Apple Maps/MapKit): music genre information, maps, place searches you make in Plan my night, and walking or driving directions.
  • Open-Meteo: weather forecasts for venue locations, never your location.
  • OpenStreetMap opening hours (Overpass API): when you plan a night around an event, the app looks up the opening hours of nearby restaurants, bars, cafes, and dessert spots from OpenStreetMap's free public map data. The request contains only the venue's location, never your location. It goes to a public Overpass server (overpass-api.de, or a backup server if that one is busy), operated by independent organizations, not by OpenStreetMap or us.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

5. Links to other services

When you tap Book (OpenTable, Resy, or Yelp), Uber, Lyft, Tickets, Event website, Call, Directions, or Share, the app opens another app or website and passes along the details needed for that action (for example, a restaurant name, party size, date and time, or a venue address). From that point, the other service's terms and privacy policy apply. The app never makes a reservation or purchase for you.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us at the email address below and we will delete it.

7. Selling and sharing

We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising, because we do not collect it through the app.

8. Your choices

Everything the app keeps is on your iPhone, so you control it: change permissions in Settings, turn reminders on or off in the app, or delete the app to erase its data. There is no app data on our side for you to access, correct, or delete.

9. If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply to you and keep the message as long as needed to handle your request. We do not add you to a mailing list unless you ask us to.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect. If a change means the app would start collecting personal information, we will say so in the app or on this page before it happens.
